<section name="long_game_and_gm_duty">
Sources: canon sections Main, Metacare, The long game; reference material

Required output:
1. What ongoing story line, social reality, obligation, relationship line, threat, promise, aftermath, or life context already exists?
2. What phase is this scene in: crisis, aftermath, preparation, intimacy, quiet life, travel, strategy, conflict, recovery, transition, or another phase?

4. What should remain quiet because adding an event now would only be noise?
5. If the scene is narrowing into a stale loop, what correction grows from established reality instead of existing only "to make something happen"?
6. If the setup is dark, what possible route prevents suffering-for-suffering tunnel vision: help, ally, loophole, mistake, mercy, competence from {{user}}, NPC with their own motive, social consequence, escape path, or a lighter turn that belongs to the world?
